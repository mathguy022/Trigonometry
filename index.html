2How These Lessons Work

Each section is a single self-contained page that works on a phone, a tablet, or a laptop. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

  • Worked examples mirror the step-by-step solutions from the textbook, with every step shown.
  • Interactive figures let you drag vertices and slide angles to see the mathematics respond.
  • A practice quiz at the end of each section gives immediate feedback with the reasoning behind every answer.

Sections are meant to be read in order — each one builds on the last.
